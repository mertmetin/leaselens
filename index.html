    What LeaseLens checks

    Common clauses that Ontario’s Residential Tenancies Act makes void: no-pets rules, damage deposits, late fees, required post-dated cheques, guest limits, entry without notice, “tenant pays all repairs”, early rent increases and more. Each result cites the section of the law and links to the official text.

    Not legal advice. LeaseLens spots common problem clauses using pattern rules; it can miss things and can’t judge your specific situation. For help, contact the Landlord and Tenant Board or a community legal clinic (Steps to Justice).
